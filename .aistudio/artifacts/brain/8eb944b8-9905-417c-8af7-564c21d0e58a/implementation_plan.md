# Single Slide Brief: Next-Gen Factory Operations

### Slide Title
**Turning Factory Data Into Fast, Profitable Decisions**

### Subtitle
*A minimalist operations cockpit that eliminates hidden production losses in real time.*

---

### The Problem (Pain Points)
- **Invisible Losses:** Plants miss daily targets without knowing if the culprit is machine downtime, bad raw materials, or slow cycle times until hours later.
- **Data Overload:** Traditional industrial screens are crammed with complex jargon and obscure numbers that cause confusion and slow down reaction times.
- **Disconnected Response:** Floor anomalies still rely on manual calls or paper notes, wasting valuable hours before corrective actions are approved.

---

### The Solution (How We Tackle It)
- **Crystal-Clear Visibility:** Live line performance on one clean screen—no confusing formulas or mystery metrics.
- **Instant Root-Cause Drilldown:** Pinpoint anomalies (equipment vs. materials vs. process) in a single click.
- **One-Click Action:** Floor supervisors can quarantine defective lots and dispatch tasks directly from the dashboard.

---

### Why It Matters (The Bottom Line)
- **Cut Unplanned Downtime by up to 35%** with immediate response times.
- **Reduce Material Waste by ~20%** by catching flawed batches before mass assembly.
- **Zero Training Required:** Built with clean UX principles so anyone on the team can operate it in minutes.
